OpenAI’s 2025 Developer Stack Converged Around Multimodal Agents

PUBLISHED 2026.09.28 3 MIN READ — VIEWS #OpenAI#AI Research

OpenAI’s year-end review describes a platform shift from isolated model calls toward multimodal, tool-using systems with durable execution and evaluation infrastructure.

Editorial summary

OpenAI's 2025 retrospective organizes the year around convergence. Reasoning became a configurable capability in general-purpose models, multimodal inputs and outputs moved into core APIs, and agent building blocks made longer workflows easier to construct and operate.

Codex is presented as both a model line and a set of working surfaces spanning CLI, cloud, IDE, safety controls, and integrations. The Responses API, Agents SDK, AgentKit, hosted tools, asynchronous execution, events, and cost controls form the surrounding platform for production agent systems.

The review also covers audio, images, video, documents, open standards, open-source components, open-weight models, evaluation, and tuning. Its recommended-model section is explicitly an end-of-2025 snapshot, not a current buying guide.

The useful historical signal is that model quality ceased to be the only platform dimension. Developers increasingly had to reason about state, tools, observability, safety, latency, and unit economics as parts of one system. Because the article is a publisher retrospective, its emphasis and taxonomy reflect OpenAI's own product strategy.
