## Editorial summary

OpenAI's GPT-5.4 is described as a major improvement in frontend design capabilities, with enhanced image understanding, stronger functional completeness, and better tool use. The model can generate visually appealing interfaces when guided with clear design constraints, visual references, and structured narratives. It is trained to use image search and generation tools, allowing it to incorporate visual reasoning directly into its design process. However, the model's output depends on the quality of prompts and guidance provided. It can produce production-ready frontends with subtle touches, well-crafted interactions, and beautiful imagery when instructed properly. Practical techniques include defining design systems upfront, providing visual references, and structuring pages as narratives. The model is also trained for computer use, enabling it to navigate interfaces and use tools like Playwright for inspection and validation. Despite these improvements, the model may still produce generic layouts if prompts are underspecified. It is advised to use low reasoning levels for simpler websites and avoid overcomplicating designs. The frontend-skill is introduced as a dedicated tool to enforce restrained composition, image-led hierarchy, and tasteful motion. However, the model's output is not guaranteed to be free of limitations, such as weak branding or UI clutter, unless explicitly addressed in the prompts.
