> Anthropic is packaging recurring small-business work into scheduled routines, connectors, and reusable skills rather than asking owners to begin every task from a blank chat.

## Editorial summary

Claude for Small Business now presents 43 workflows and 27 new integrations spanning commerce, finance, marketing, payroll, collaboration, and customer operations. The product direction is to turn recurring work into named routines that can be scheduled, repeated, and adapted.

The article organizes examples around the cadence of running a company: daily prioritization, customer and sales work, weekly marketing, and month-end close. When a native connector is absent, the proposed paths include exported data, Zapier, or a purpose-built connector, which broadens reach but also shifts more integration responsibility to the owner.

Customer stories show founders training skills on their voice, reconciling large transaction sets, and converting manual reporting into repeatable dashboards. These are illustrative, publisher-selected outcomes; they do not establish typical savings across the diverse small-business market.

The meaningful shift is from isolated assistance to an operating rhythm. That can reduce setup friction, but businesses still need to decide which records are authoritative, who approves financial or customer-facing actions, and how connected services are monitored when schemas or permissions change.
