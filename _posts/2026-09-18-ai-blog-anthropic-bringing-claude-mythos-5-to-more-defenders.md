## Editorial summary

Anthropic is expanding access to its Claude Mythos 5 model for cybersecurity applications, integrating it into existing tools used by defenders of critical infrastructure. The company has also launched the Defender Advantage Fund (0xDAF) with $35 million in credits to support open-source security efforts. Additionally, Anthropic is expanding its Cyber Verification Program to provide more organizations with access to dual-use capabilities on models like Opus and Sonnet, with plans to include Mythos-class access in the future. The company emphasizes that these initiatives aim to provide defensive capabilities while maintaining safety controls to prevent misuse. Claude Security scans are now available on Claude Mythos 5 for Enterprise customers, allowing them to detect vulnerabilities and suggest patches without exposing the model directly to users. However, the company acknowledges limitations, including the need for human review of patches and the risk of misuse if safeguards are not properly implemented. Anthropic also highlights exceptions, such as the requirement for organizations to meet strict security control requirements to access Mythos-class models through Project Glasswing. The company is in the early stages of these efforts and expects them to evolve over time.
