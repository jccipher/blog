## Editorial summary

Anthropic's Claude for Small Business initiative aimed to bring AI tools to small businesses, which contribute significantly to the U.S. economy but have been underserved in AI adoption. Through workshops and training, Anthropic gathered insights from over 1,000 small business owners, revealing both the opportunities and challenges of AI integration. The initiative included free workshops in cities like Chicago, Tulsa, Dallas, and others, where small business owners learned to use AI tools like Claude Cowork to automate back-office tasks. Key lessons included how AI can level the playing field for non-technical entrepreneurs, the importance of data security and governance, the need for AI fluency training, and the value of peer learning. While many owners saw AI as a valuable tool for saving time and resources, concerns about accuracy, data privacy, and the learning curve remained. Anthropic plans to expand its efforts through the Claude SMB Trainer Program and future tours, aiming to provide more support and community for small businesses adopting AI.
