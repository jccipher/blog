> T. Rowe Price is incorporating Claude into its investment workflow to support research, multi-step processes, and tool development. The firm aims to improve efficiency and decision-making by using Claude's capabilities, while maintaining human judgment as central to its investment process.

## Editorial summary

T. Rowe Price has expanded the use of Anthropic's Claude across its investment operations, integrating it into research, multi-step processes, and tool development. The firm aims to enhance the capacity of portfolio managers and analysts by using Claude to synthesize complex information, manage knowledge-intensive tasks, and build tools that support investment teams. This initiative aligns with T. Rowe Price's AI leadership model, which places AI leaders within its investment and global distribution teams and assigns responsibility for AI evaluation and scaling to T. Rowe Price Labs. Each application is governed by the firm's standards for responsible AI use, ensuring that human judgment remains central to client outcomes. According to Peter Nolan, Head of Asset and Wealth Management at Anthropic, most enterprise AI deployments begin with the back office, but T. Rowe Price started with the people who select securities. Eric Veiel, President and Co-head of Global Investments at T. Rowe Price, described Claude as an extension of the firm's long-standing research process, enabling investment professionals to cover more ground and go deeper on critical issues. The firm's broader AI efforts include training, talent development, and responsible-use guidance to ensure associates can confidently use AI while maintaining client focus and human judgment.
