## Editorial summary

Anthropic's 'The AI-Native SDLC Playbook' presents a comprehensive guide to adapting the software development lifecycle (SDLC) for an AI-driven world. The article argues that traditional SDLC processes, which were designed around human limitations, are no longer effective as AI can now generate code at unprecedented speeds. The key claim is that the SDLC must be reimagined to align with AI's capabilities, ensuring that human oversight remains central to governance. The article outlines six stages of the AI-native SDLC—Plan, Design, Build, Test, Deploy, and Maintain—each with specific practices and tools to integrate AI effectively. It emphasizes that AI should not replace human judgment but should be embedded into the process to enhance productivity and governance. The article also highlights the importance of version control, artifact-based auditing, and the use of AI-native tools like Claude Code to streamline development. However, it acknowledges limitations, such as the need for human review in regulated environments and the challenges of scaling AI-native practices across large organizations. The editorial concludes that while AI-native SDLC offers significant productivity gains, it requires careful implementation and governance to ensure compliance and quality.
